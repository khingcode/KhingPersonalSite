      1. v2.1my roomlatesta cozy little page of its own
        • a new house icon in the corner leads to my room, a separate page you can walk into
        • click the grand piano and a piano you can play pops up: tap the keys, slide across them, or use your keyboard. there's a little tune too, and the chibi reacts
        • the house asks for a password before you come in
        • click the bulletin board to open it: photo memories (photos are on the way) and a pinned bible verse
        • the room fits on one screen, with a cozy furnished room: bookshelf, desk, armchair, a wall clock and more
        • click the window to switch between day and night
      2. v2.0VOTE ON THE DOMAINyour vote picks my web address
        • VOTE FOR THE DOMAIN OF THE FULL LAUNCH! CHOOSE KHINGKONG.COM OR SUPREMEKHING.COM, OR SUGGEST YOUR OWN. FIND IT IN THE WORK WINDOW, UNDER DEVELOPMENT.
        • ONE VOTE EACH. THE RESULTS SHOW AFTER YOU VOTE.
      3. v1.9stamps, songs, hearts, stickers & a secretlinks, the guestbook and a little surprise
        • links: instagram, ko-fi and merch have a “coming soon” stamp until they're ready
        • guestbook notes can carry a song from spotify or youtube, and the site's piano mutes itself while one plays
        • you can now heart guestbook notes and replies
        • guestbook notes can be given a color and a little sticker, and the guestbook now sits side by side: write on the left, read on the right
        • spam the little guy past his dark side and something happens… can you bring him back? hint: look to the night sky
      4. v1.8contact, work & hellosnew windows, new greetings and a fresh about
        • the little guy now holds out a letter in the contact window
        • a big send me an email button that opens your mail app
        • work window: a tools list, a development list and a first project card
        • a small red number on the devlog icon when there's something new
        • the little guy says good morning, afternoon or evening based on your clock
        • he now says hello every so often while you're on the page, not just when you click him
        • he has a new thing to say when you click him
        • about got a bigger, roomier layout and a new intro with a few highlighted words
      5. v1.7devlogyou're looking at it
        • a new devlog icon on the home screen
        • a simple list of everything added to the site, newest first
        • tap a version to open or close it
      6. v1.6seasonsthe site dresses up for the holidays
        • christmas (dec 1–26): falling snow and a santa hat on the little guy
        • valentine's (feb 10–14): floating hearts
        • spring (mar 20–apr 20): drifting blossom petals and a flower clip
        • my birthday (jun 13–15): confetti, balloons and a party hat
        • each one switches on by itself, then goes away after
      7. v1.5the little guy has feelingshe reacts when you poke him a lot
        • click him too fast and he gets angry: he turns red, shakes and grumbles
        • keep going… and something else might happen 👀
        • he calms down on his own and says sorry

        • notes are saved online, so everyone sees the same guestbook
